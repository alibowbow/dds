## 2026-08-02 - [Korean Typography and Readability]
**Learning:** Proper typographic styling, like `word-break: keep-all;` and using web fonts like 'Pretendard', drastically improves text readability in Korean by preventing breaks in the middle of words and maintaining aesthetic consistency.
**Action:** Always ensure web fonts are correctly imported via CDN or locally and applying `word-break: keep-all;` paired with `overflow-wrap: break-word;` for proper rendering in Korean-heavy interfaces.
